// 01. about

Who I am

My deep dive into AI started with a very real, very Riga problem: finding a parking spot in the morning. A friend and I kept hitting the same frustration every day on the way to work. Instead of just complaining, we decided to actually do something about it. That became the AI Parking Detection project, and the rabbit hole that pulled me much deeper into AI than I'd ever been before.

Right now I work as Head of Corporate Sales at AI Master Lab, where my role sits at the intersection of business and AI — talking to C-level clients, identifying where AI can genuinely help them, and translating that into something actionable. Before that, my background was varied: telecoms sales, solar panel installation in the Netherlands, managing a car wash. Everything I've built with AI, the parking detection system, the email classifier, the n8n automations, I did on my own time out of pure curiosity. Nobody paid me for it. Currently learning Python properly via Codefinity, and I spent around 3 to 5 months studying UI/UX design at Riga Coding School, having already taught myself the basics before that.

As a person, I'm an optimist with a competitive streak in everything I do. I boulder, play basketball, occasionally game, and in winter I enjoy car sports like drifting. My wife says I'm funny, sometimes cringe. I'm curious about everything and want to try everything. AI feels that way to me, there's still so much uncharted territory. It gives me the same feeling as JARVIS in Iron Man or the docking scene in Interstellar. We're just getting started.

AI Email Classifier
Streamlit web app that classifies emails across 5 dimensions using Claude API, with an eval-driven model selection process.
  • Classifies: category, priority, sentiment, one-line summary, recommended action
  • Built a 25-email eval set with hand-assigned ground truth. Sonnet hit 96% accuracy vs 84% for Haiku
  • Documented cost/latency tradeoffs: $0.053/run (Sonnet) vs $0.017/run (Haiku)
  • Deployed on Streamlit Cloud, live demo available with English + Latvian examples
